01 · About

Reading environmental change through space and time.

I am a Geological Engineer graduated from the Escuela Superior Politécnica del Litoral (ESPOL), Ecuador, and I hold a Master’s degree in Remote Sensing from the Instituto Nacional de Pesquisas Espaciais (INPE), Brazil.

My master’s thesis, Multi-scale Analysis of Socio-Environmental Vulnerability under Mining Pressure in the Brazilian Amazon, reflects my research focus on the environmental and territorial impacts of extractive activities in the Amazon.

I am passionate about contributing to the environmental sustainability of the mining, oil and electricity industries through scientific research and applications of remote sensing, geographic information systems and Earth observation.

02 · Research

Featured research themes

Projects connect remote sensing with practical questions about ecosystems, extractive activity and river-system change.

01

Project 1 · Jan — Dec 2026 · Ecuadorian Amazon

Illegal Mining Pressure & Forest Degradation Zoning

A geospatial project focused on identifying areas at highest risk of illegal mining and deforestation in the southern Ecuadorian Amazon through multi-criteria spatial analysis and high-resolution cartography.

Funded by the European Union through the Amazonía+ Programme (Amazonía Viva), and carried out by FICT-ESPOL with COOPI in consortium with USFQ and UNAD.

02

Project 2 · Apr — Dec 2024 · Ecuadorian Amazon

AMAZONAS

The AMAZONAS Project evaluates the effects of illegal mining and deforestation through geospatial intelligence, remote sensing and multitemporal mapping.

The study supports territorial governance, public policy design and the reduction of socio-environmental vulnerability. Funded by Corporación Participación Ciudadana with support from the U.S. Embassy in Ecuador.

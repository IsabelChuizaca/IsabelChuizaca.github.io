01 · About

Reading environmental change through space and time.

I am a Geological Engineer from ESPOL with a research focus on remote sensing, geographic information systems and Earth observation.

My work examines environmental and territorial transformations in the Amazon, especially those associated with mining, land-use change, river dynamics and large infrastructure projects. I combine satellite imagery, spatial analysis and geoscience to turn complex environmental processes into measurable evidence.
